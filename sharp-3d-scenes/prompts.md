## Pro Tips

1. **Always include**: "photorealistic", "depth of field", "8k" or "ultra detailed"
2. **Add lighting**: "volumetric fog", "golden hour", "dramatic lighting", "soft light"
3. **Specify perspective**: "wide angle", "aerial view", "eye level", "low angle"
4. **Include depth cues**: Mention foreground AND background elements
5. **Avoid**: Flat illustrations, pure graphic design, text-heavy images
